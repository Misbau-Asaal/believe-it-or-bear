Napoleon conquered half of Europe. Then he lost a battle to bunnies.

Legend says that in 1807, to celebrate a peace treaty, he wanted a rabbit hunt.

His chief of staff, Berthier, rounded up hundreds of rabbits.

The hunters took aim. The cages opened.

But the rabbits didn't run away. They ran straight at Napoleon.

Nobody had caught wild rabbits. They'd bought tame ones from farmers.

And these were hungry. To them, people meant dinner.

They swarmed his boots and climbed his legs. His men swung sticks. The coachmen cracked whips.

Then the rabbits split in two and came around both sides.

Napoleon ran for his carriage.

The greatest general of his age, outflanked by bunnies.
