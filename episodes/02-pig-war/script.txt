Two empires nearly went to war. Over one pig.

In 1859, an American farmer on San Juan Island found a pig digging up his garden. He shot it.

The pig belonged to the British.

Things escalated fast. The Americans sent soldiers. The British sent warships.

Soon, hundreds of troops and dozens of cannons faced each other across one small island.

Nobody fired.

For twelve years, both armies just camped there. Same island. Opposite ends.

Finally, a German emperor was asked to settle the border. His panel sided with the Americans.

The final death toll of the Pig War?

One pig.
