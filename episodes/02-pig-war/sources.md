# 02 The Pig War: sources and fact notes

Source: National Park Service, San Juan Island National Historical Park, "The Pig War"
https://www.nps.gov/sajh/learn/historyculture/the-pig-war.htm

- June 15, 1859: American Lyman Cutlar shot a Hudson's Bay Company pig rooting in his garden.
- By August 31, 1859: 461 Americans, 14 field cannons and eight 32-pounder naval guns; British warships with 52 guns.
- November 1859: joint military occupation agreed; it lasted about 12 years.
- 1872: dispute referred to Kaiser Wilhelm I, who passed it to a three-man commission; ruled for the US on October 21, 1872.
- No human casualties; the only casualty was the pig.
- Left out on purpose: the popular story about how much money the farmer offered for the pig (not verified).
