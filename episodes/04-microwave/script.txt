Legend says the microwave oven was invented by a melted chocolate bar.

In 1945, Percy Spencer built radar parts.

One day he stood next to a running radar tube. Something in his pocket felt warm.

His chocolate bar had melted.

Most people would complain. Percy got curious.

He held popcorn up to the tube. It popped all over the lab.

Then he tried an egg. A coworker leaned in close.

The egg exploded in his face.

The tube's invisible waves shake the water in food. Shaking makes heat.

The first microwave oven weighed seven hundred fifty pounds.

Percy's bonus for the patent? Two dollars.
