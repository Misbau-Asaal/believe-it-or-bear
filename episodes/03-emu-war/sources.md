# 03 The Emu War: sources and fact notes

- Britannica, "Emu War": https://www.britannica.com/topic/Emu-War
- National Geographic, "The bizarre story of when Australia went to war with emus, and lost": https://www.nationalgeographic.com/history/article/australian-emu-war-history

- November to December 1932, Campion district, Western Australia.
- About 20,000 emus migrating into wheat farms during a drought; many farmers were WWI veterans (soldier settlers) who petitioned for help.
- The army sent Maj. G. P. W. Meredith and two soldiers of the Royal Australian Artillery with two Lewis machine guns and 10,000 rounds (Britannica).
- The emus scattered into small groups when fired on; one bird per group acted as a lookout (Britannica). A gun jammed during an ambush at a watering hole (National Geographic).
- Fewer than 1,000 emus reported killed (Meredith's report: 986); figures are disputed, so the script says "fewer than a thousand".
- Meredith said the emus could face machine guns with the invulnerability of tanks (paraphrased in the script).
- The operation was called off; the government later turned to bounties and fencing.
- Wording note: Australia did not formally declare war, so the script says "went to war".
