In 1932, Australia went to war. With birds.

About twenty thousand emus were eating the wheat farms of Western Australia.

The farmers were war veterans. So they asked the army for help.

The army sent soldiers. And two machine guns.

The emus had a plan.

They split into small groups. They posted lookouts. They ran.

The soldiers fired thousands of bullets. One machine gun jammed.

After weeks of fighting, they had killed fewer than a thousand birds.

Their commander said the emus could face machine guns like tanks.

The army went home.

The emus stayed.
