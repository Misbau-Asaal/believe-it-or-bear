The Polish army had a bear. And the bear had a rank.

In nineteen forty-two, soldiers found an orphaned cub in Iran. They adopted him. They named him Wojtek.

He grew up wrestling the men and sharing their camp.

Then came an order: no pets on the transport ship.

So the soldiers enlisted him. Private Wojtek got a paybook, and a seat on the boat.

At Monte Cassino, the men hauled heavy crates of shells. Wojtek watched, stood up, and joined in. By every account, he never dropped one.

The army promoted him to corporal.

The rule said no pets. Nobody had written a rule about corporals.
