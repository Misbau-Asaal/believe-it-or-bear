# Believe It Or Bear: channel copy

Handle: Believe It Or Bear on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

## Bios

- YouTube description: True stories that sound made up, told in under a minute by a bear in an army cap. Strange history, odd animals, unbelievable rules, and a twist at the end of every one. New shorts every week.
- Instagram (150 max): True stories. Twist endings. 🐻 Strange history in under a minute. New shorts every week ⬇️
- TikTok (80 max): True stories. Twist endings. 🐻 Strange history in 60 seconds.

## Episode 01 post

- YouTube title: The army banned pets. So they made the bear a soldier. 🐻
- Caption: In 1942, Polish soldiers adopted an orphaned bear cub named Wojtek. When the rules said no pets, they found a loophole. Believe it… or bear. #history #ww2 #wojtek #truestory #animals #shorts

## Posting rules

- Same video to all three platforms on the same day.
- 2-3 shorts a week; consistency over volume.
- No bought views, no follow-for-follow.
- Every episode is a different true story with its own structure and writing, so the channel never looks like one template (YouTube's "inauthentic content" policy).
- Fact-check every claim; anything that can't be verified gets softened ("by every account") or cut.
