Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

The Believe It Or Bear Uploader is used only by the owner of the Believe It Or Bear YouTube channel.

What it accesses. With the owner's permission (Google OAuth, scope youtube.upload), it uploads videos and their titles, descriptions and tags to the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not read, collect or store any other Google account data, and it does not access anyone else's data.

Storage. The access credentials are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's GitHub repository and are used only to upload the channel's videos. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Revoking access. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This tool uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact: through the Believe It Or Bear YouTube channel.
