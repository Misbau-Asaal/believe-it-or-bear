# Lineup

One episode a day, live at 6 PM New York time (7 AM Malaysia the next morning). The days live in `episodes/schedule.json`; the render run reads its episode's day from there, so **Render episode** with `publish: auto` is the only click. If an episode isn't ready in time, it takes the next day nobody else has.

## Reserve

- The Great Stink (1858): Parliament fixed London's sewers in 18 days once they had to smell the river.
- Dagen H (1967): Sweden switched sides of the road overnight, and accidents went down.
- The Toledo War (1835): Michigan lost Toledo, got the Upper Peninsula, which was full of copper.

Every hook and twist is checked again when the script is written; anything that can't be confirmed is cut or told as legend.
